# Captions and cutaways

`refine-talking-reels` owns the edit and integration. For a caption-only request or a compatible verbatim caption layer during a recut, use `embedded-captions` when available. For translated subtitles, preserve the timing and meaning of spoken phrases on the selected edit, then render them with a compatible caption workflow.

Use `motion-graphics` for a standalone animated insert and `hyperframes-animation` for motion recipes when that toolchain fits. For a cutaway in a recut, choose the spoken beat, duration, and return to the speaker in the reel timeline. A draft chat bubble that types and deletes on the misunderstanding line is one example of action that conveys the meaning. Choose the number of inserts by reviewing the whole reel, including the stretches between them. Prefer continuous dialogue under a visual cutaway unless the requested edit calls for a change.

For animated captions, choose a consistent type treatment, placement, and entrance for supporting phrases. Use color, scale, a paper card, or an underline for selected beats. Restraint at the payoff can mean a calmer entrance and longer hold within the same treatment. Check legibility against speaker footage and every insert, including the shortest cues and the platform overlay areas.

## Handoff

- Identify the selected take, cut version, and asset path. Give the source range for a trim or insert taken from footage.
- Give the start and end on the output timeline, its frame rate and dimensions, the spoken phrase and its transcript range, the caption language and whether it is verbatim or translated, and any uncertain wording or timing.
- Describe the intended visual action and safe areas. Request a timed editable caption layer or a cutaway asset with its duration, framing, and audio requirements. Use a transparent overlay when the graphic needs to sit over the speaker.
- After any trim, compare the caption and insert boundaries with the revised timeline. Preserve an editable timeline or caption file. Review a regular cue, an emphasized cue, and an insert on the export.
